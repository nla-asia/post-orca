# PostOrca

PostOrca is a Mac app for founders who build in public. Orca, its AI agent, learns your product and how you write, then helps you create content about what you ship and grow your audience on X.

**[Download PostOrca for macOS](https://github.com/nla-asia/post-orca/releases/latest/download/PostOrca-arm64.dmg)** · [Website](https://orca.atpa.site) · [All releases](https://github.com/nla-asia/post-orca/releases)

## What Orca does

- **Content from what you build.** Orca reads your project and recent commits and turns real work into post ideas, posts in your voice, and product videos recorded from your running app.
- **Posts that sound like you.** Orca learns your voice from your own posts and from the changes you ask for, so drafts need fewer edits over time.
- **A growth plan.** Agree on a goal and a posting rhythm for each account, then get a plan for each day.
- **Replies worth posting.** Orca finds fresh posts in your X feed that are worth replying to and drafts a reply in your voice. Drafts appear right under the post on X, and a Draft reply button lets you ask for one on any post.
- **Watchlists.** Keep lists of people you learn from or want to connect with, and see their labels next to their posts on X.
- **Routines.** Work Orca does on its own every day, like checking what changed in your projects or how your posts did.

You stay in control: you review every draft and post replies yourself.

## Requirements

- macOS on Apple Silicon.
- A PostOrca account. Create one in the app.
- For browser and X features: Google Chrome with the PostOrca Browser Bridge extension.

## Install

1. Download [PostOrca-arm64.dmg](https://github.com/nla-asia/post-orca/releases/latest/download/PostOrca-arm64.dmg).
2. Open it and drag PostOrca to Applications.
3. Open PostOrca and create your account.

The app is signed and notarized by Apple.

## How it fits together

- **The desktop app** is where you talk with Orca and where its work runs: reading your projects, recording your app, and keeping your drafts, plans and routines.
- **The browser extension** connects Chrome to the desktop app on your computer. It lets Orca work in tabs it opens, using your existing sign-ins, and shows your reply drafts and watchlist labels on X. It never posts, likes or follows on its own.
- **Your PostOrca account** keeps your brand profile, voice, plans and drafts in sync.
